The Beginning

A Family's Commitment to Chicago

Sumit Construction Company, Inc. was founded in 1985 with a belief that quality craftsmanship and honest business would always speak louder than anything else. From a single family in Chicago's Hermosa neighborhood, we have grown into one of the city's most recognized public works contractors — completing over 600 projects across Chicago's streets, alleys, and public facilities.


As a family-owned and operated business, we take personal pride in every project we sign our name to. Our leadership has been hands-on from the very beginning — working alongside crews, building relationships with city agencies, and ensuring that Sumit's word is its bond.
